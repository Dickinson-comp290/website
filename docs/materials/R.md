## Reading & Discussions

Throughout the semester we will have days designated for Reading and Discussion. On these days we will spend approximately half of the class discussing a contemporary issue in computing or tech with social, legal and/or ethical implications and which has some connection to our course content.

To prepare for these discussions you will be assigned a few short readings and given some discussion questions to seed our in class conversation. You are expected to complete the readings and create a page of notes that summarize your thoughts on the discussion questions highlighting any particular points that you would like to make during the discussion. **Unless otherwise specified, no electronic devices will be permitted during the discussions. So bring a hard copy of your notes to class.** Your notes will be collected at the end of class period, lightly reviewed, and returned to you in the following class period. Note that **no AI use is permitted for summarizing the readings or in the creation of the notes.**

Each of the reading assignments and associated discussion questions are given below.

### RD01 - Education, AI and "Cognitive Surrender"

- Read the article "An M.I.T. Report Warns that A.I. Is Causing 'Cognitive Surrender.' Universities Are in a Bind."  (Posted in the course Teams Channel.)

Prepare for a discussion including but not limited to the following questions:

1. Has the presence of AI affected your _help seeking_ behaviors? How so? Which of the changes have been positive or negative?
2. What do you see as the purpose of doing homework assignments, papers and projects? Does AI change that purpose in your mind?
3. Is it the instructor's responsibility to "A.I. Proof" assignments? Is that even possible?
4. "What is OK to offload to A.I., and what is not?" Why?
5. When if ever do you think bans on AI make sense? Why?
6. What responsibilities do you see faculty / schools having or not having with regards to A.I.?
7. What do they mean by the phrase "Cognitive Surrender"? How can you prevent it?
8. What else would you like to hear about from your classmates on this topic?
